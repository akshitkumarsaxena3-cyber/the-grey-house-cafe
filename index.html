WELCOME TO THE GREY HOUSE

An Aesthetic Oasis Created For Cherished Memories

Perched elegantly on the 3rd Floor of Dua Tower in Janakpuri, The Grey House has redefined modern social dining in Bareilly.

Whether you are planning an intimate candle-lit date night under the open sky, an energetic birthday bash with friends, a relaxed family dinner, or an exquisite kitty party, our versatile multi-zoned venue is designed to give you an experience that touches your heart.

Breathtaking Multi-Zoned Ambiance

Choose between the fresh breezes of our Open Terrace, the bohemian greens of the Courtyard, or the plush comfort of our Indoor Lounge.

Finest Culinary Ingredients

From charcoal-smoked Paneer Angara & juicy Tandoori Chicken to authentic Kurkure Momos & creamy Italian pastas, every dish is prepared fresh to order.

Warm & Attentive Hospitality

Our cooperative staff and dedicated event managers ensure you and your guests feel treated like royalty from the moment you step in.
